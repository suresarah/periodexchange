    Period care is basic care. — The Period Exchange promise

    We support every side of the cycle

    Period Exchange pays for free pads and tampons through brand sponsorship. Companies advertise on the packaging, and that covers the cost of making and delivering the products.

    • Products come in white-label packaging
    • Brands sponsor the wrapper design
    • Ad revenue pays for production and delivery
    • Partners and the people they serve pay nothing

    The result is a funding loop that doesn't depend on donations, or on the people using the products.

    The donation model

    • Relies on fundraising drives
    • Supply comes and goes
    • Heavy admin work for volunteers
    • A short-term fix

    Donations matter, but they're unpredictable. When a drive ends, the shelves empty out again.

    The exchange model

    • Funded by sponsorship revenue
    • Steady, predictable supply
    • Easy to scale to new locations
    • Lasting infrastructure

    Because sponsors pay for each run, supply keeps flowing. Every new sponsor means more places stocked.
